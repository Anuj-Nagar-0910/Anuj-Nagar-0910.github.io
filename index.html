Experiment 01 · flagship

MF4 Log Analytics

Two processors, two clocks, hundreds of power-ups. For every start-up in a multi-gigabyte recording, which one came first, by how many milliseconds, and why.

95 h → 2.5 h

From about 95 hours to about 2.5 hours. Estimated for a 10-recording campaign (about 585 start-ups). The tool time was measured once; the manual time is my estimate of 10 minutes per cycle.

  1. 01LoadOpen multi-GB MF4 files lazily
  2. 02SegmentSplit into power-ups
  3. 03MeasureResets, gaps, first app update
  4. 04CorrectRemove clock offset between paths
  5. 05Cross-checkA second, offset-free method
  6. 06JudgeOK, NOK, CHECK or INFO
  7. 07ReportCSV plus browser dashboard
